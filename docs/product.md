# Product Overview

## The problem

Finding relevant nightlife often means searching across venue pages, promoter accounts, ticketing platforms, social feeds, and generic event listings. The information exists, but discovery is fragmented.

AfterFive is designed around a simpler consumer question:

**What is actually worth doing after five?**

## Product direction

The experience prioritizes culture-led nightlife rather than trying to become a generic directory of every event.

Core product themes include:

- strong event relevance
- fast multi-city discovery
- useful event imagery and details
- curated and featured surfaces
- operational quality behind the consumer experience
- tools that make event management sustainable as coverage grows

## Building beyond the consumer UI

A discovery product is only as good as its underlying event data.

That makes ingestion, moderation, ownership, recurring events, editing, readiness checks, and admin workflows part of the product—not back-office afterthoughts.

The production implementation remains private, but this case study documents the system-level thinking behind the product.
